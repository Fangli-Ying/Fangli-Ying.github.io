Dr. Fangli Ying

Dr. Fangli Ying serves as a Lecturer, Master’s Supervisor and Co-Supervisor of Doctoral Students in the Department of Computer Science, East China University of Science and Technology (ECUST). He also heads the industrial-research cooperation director in the Department of Computer Science. His research centers on artificial intelligence and interdisciplinary convergence, addressing practical industrial challenges via cross-disciplinary exploration. His current research directions cover computer vision, quantum error correction, financial trading agent, and deep generative models. He founded the Industry-University Joint Innovation Center on Quantum computing and AI. The industrial enzyme optimization design project developed through his industrial collaboration was listed among the Top 10 Quantum Computing Application Cases by the Shanghai Science and Technology Commission in 2025.

Research

Our group focuses on interdisciplinary artificial intelligence research that bridges computer science, bioengineering, finance and quantum information, with an emphasis on solving real industrial problems. Our core research pillars cover four interconnected directions: 1) advanced computer vision including generative models, few-shot meta-learning, computational aesthetics, 3D human reconstruction and industrial medical visual inspection; 2) computational bioinformatics leveraging deep generative networks to design functional proteins, antimicrobial peptides and optimize industrial fermentation workflows; 3) deep reinforcement learning for algorithmic asset trading and portfolio management; and 4) AI algorithms dedicated to quantum error correction. We closely integrate academic research with industry-university-research cooperation, translate our algorithms into practical industrial solutions, and regularly publish original works in top-tier CCF-listed journals and conferences across multimedia, machine learning and artificial intelligence.
